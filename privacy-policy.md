# KidLock Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 8, 2026.*

KidLock is made by Prosperity Simplified. This policy covers the KidLock iPhone app.

## The short version

KidLock collects nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server. Everything you set up stays on your
iPhone, and nothing about you or your child is sent to Prosperity Simplified or anyone else.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Your settings**: your child's first name (optional), age range, unlock choices, your secret phrase, favorite
  FaceTime contacts you add, and your checklist ticks. They're saved only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage.
- **Your parent PIN** is never stored. Only a salted, one-way fingerprint (hash) of it is kept, in your iPhone's
  Keychain. It stays on this device and isn't moved to a new iPhone.
- **Session history**: the last 30 lock sessions (mode, length, how many taps were blocked), on your iPhone only.
- **Videos and photos you choose** for KidLock are copied into the app's private storage on your iPhone. KidLock sees
  only the items you pick in Apple's photo picker, never the rest of your library.
- "Delete all KidLock data" in Settings, or deleting the app, removes all of this.

## Microphone and speech

If you turn on voice unlock, KidLock listens for your secret phrase **only while a lock session is running or while you
practice it**. iPhone shows an orange dot whenever the microphone is on. KidLock asks iPhone to recognize speech **on
the device**, and turns voice unlock off if your iPhone can't do that for your language. Audio is never recorded,
saved or sent by KidLock. Apple's own speech services are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## Face ID

Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. KidLock only receives "yes, it's the owner" or "no". It never sees your face data.

## Volume buttons

To recognize your volume-button unlock, KidLock watches whether the system volume goes up or down while a session is
on screen. It doesn't record anything.

## Children

KidLock is a tool for parents and caregivers. It doesn't collect personal information from children (or anyone),
contains no advertising, and has no links to purchases. Optional details you enter about your child stay on your
iPhone.

## TestFlight pilot

If you test KidLock through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with
Prosperity Simplified, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. Please don't include photos or videos of your child
in feedback.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: TestFlight testers can write
to us from TestFlight → KidLock → Send Beta Feedback; everyone else can use the support contact on KidLock's App Store
page.
