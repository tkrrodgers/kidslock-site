# Get Ready to Save the World: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 10, 2026.*

Get Ready to Save the World is a story game made by Prosperity Simplified. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad
game, including test versions sent through Apple's TestFlight.

## The short version

The game collects nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no server, and it never
connects to the internet. Nothing about you is sent to Prosperity Simplified or anyone else.

## What the game stores, and where

- **Your saved game**: your progress through the stages, the choices you made, Memory Book pages, in-game money and
  your settings. They're saved in one file in the game's private storage on your device.
- Deleting the game removes the save.

## Camera, microphone and photos

The game doesn't use the camera, the microphone or your photo library. The game engine includes the standard
descriptions for these permissions, but the game never asks for them.

## Children

The game is suitable for young players. It doesn't collect personal information from children (or anyone), contains
no advertising, and has no links to purchases or outside websites.

## TestFlight

If you test the game through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with
Prosperity Simplified, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: TestFlight testers can write
to us from TestFlight → Get Ready to Save the World → Send Beta Feedback; everyone else can use the support contact on
the game's App Store page.
